How it works

From a shipping label to an ad-ready audience, in three steps.

No marketplace API keys, no integrations to configure. Just the label PDFs you already download.

Upload your label PDFs

Drop in the shipping-label PDFs from any marketplace — one at a time or hundreds at once. The marketplace is detected automatically per file.

We read every field

Order ID, amount, COD vs. Prepaid, destination pincode, and order date — parsed directly from the label, right in your browser.

Get your dashboard

Regional split, payment mix, order timing, and an estimated gender split — formatted for Meta and Google Ads targeting decisions.
